## Candidates to check (found 2026-10-14, not yet verified)

- City of Worcester DPW, Bid 8691-J6, Water Main Improvements, Chandler and May Street (specs and pricing sheets PDF):
  https://www.worcesterma.gov/sites/default/files/bids/8691-J6%20-%20Water%20Main%20Improvements-Chandler%2BMay%20Street-DPW%20-%20Specs%2BPricing%20Sheets.pdf
  Looks like a unit-price water main job with liquidated damages. Roughly 80 pages, some broken words in the text (a good "messy" test). Check the pricing sheets are included.
- Engineering-firm advertisements often link to the full documents, for example the Milford Court Street Water Main Improvements advertisement (Tata & Howard). Follow the link to the plan room and read its terms first.

## Where to look

- COMMBUYS (commbuys.com): search "water main", "sewer", "drainage", "pump station".
- Town and city websites: Purchasing, Procurement or Bids pages; water and sewer departments.
- MassDOT advertised construction projects (roadway and drainage).
- Plan rooms used by towns and engineering firms (some need a free sign-up; read the terms).
- Search words: "water main replacement contract documents", "sewer rehabilitation bid documents",
  "Chapter 30 Section 39M", "Invitation for Bids" plus a town name.
